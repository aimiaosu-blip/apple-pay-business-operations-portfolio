# Project Lifecycle: Regional Merchant Launch

| Phase | Milestone | Exit criterion | Primary risk | Dependency | Governance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-launch | Metric contract signed | Numerators, denominators, timezone and owners approved | Definition drift | Analytics + Ops | Weekly design review |
| Pre-launch | Partner readiness | Issuer and merchant test cases pass | External delay | Partner teams | RAID log |
| Launch | Controlled rollout | Guardrails green for pilot population | Success-rate regression | Engineering | Daily launch room |
| Launch | Scale decision | Success and activation above thresholds | Biased early cohort | Complete window | Go/no-go memo |
| Post-launch | Stabilization | Seven days within reliability band | Hidden segment issue | Monitoring | Weekly review |
| Post-launch | Closeout | Actions completed and runbook updated | Learning not retained | All owners | Retrospective |

Status governance uses RAG plus explicit evidence, owner, due date, dependency and next decision. A red status without a named action is incomplete.
