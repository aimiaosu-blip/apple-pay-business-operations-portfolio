# Weekly / Monthly Business Review Pack

## Executive summary
The synthetic portfolio grows through 2025 while aggregate success rate remains high. A deliberately planted, bounded incident from 15 Sep to 5 Oct produces a visible dip. Drill-down attributes the decline to East China / Issuer B / Transit / Older phone rather than a portfolio-wide demand shift.

## Review cadence
1. KPI scorecard: attempts, success rate, failure rate, synthetic GMV.
2. Funnel: activation, first transaction, 30-day repeat by cohort.
3. Reliability cuts: region, issuer, category and device.
4. Exceptions: threshold breaches and data-quality tests.
5. Action tracker: owner, due date, validation metric and status.

## Decision rule
Escalate when a segment with at least 500 weekly attempts is >2 percentage points below its trailing four-week baseline, then validate denominator stability and upstream data completeness before assigning operational cause.
